In this paper, we have reviewed 135 works to assess the extent to which current open-source toolkits, frameworks, and authoring tools can effectively support the development of components tailored to urban visual analytics, identifying strengths and limitations in addressing the unique challenges posed by urban data.

The paper can be found [here](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cag.2024.104013).
