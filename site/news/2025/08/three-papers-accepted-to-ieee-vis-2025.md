Happy to share that our group has had **three full papers accepted at IEEE VIS 2025**!

**Urbanite**, led by UIC PhD candidate Gustavo Moreira, introduces a framework for human–AI collaboration in urban visual analytics, using a dataflow-based model that lets users specify intent at multiple levels, aligning specification, process, and evaluation while supporting explainability and interaction provenance.  
More at [https://urbantk.org/urbanite](/urbanite/).

[![](/media/news/three-papers-accepted-to-ieee-vis-2025/logo-white.webp)](/urbanite/)

**VA-Blueprint**, led by UIC PhD student Leonardo Ferreira, presents a methodology and knowledge base that uses LLMs to systematically capture the building blocks of visual analytics systems, revealing design patterns through a hierarchical blueprint to enable more structured, reproducible, and efficient VA development.  
More at [https://urbantk.org/va-blueprint](/va-blueprint/).

[![](/media/news/three-papers-accepted-to-ieee-vis-2025/logo_gray_background_grid.webp)](/va-blueprint/)

**StreetWeave**, led by UIC PhD candidate Sanjana Srabanti, offers a declarative grammar for creating custom, street-overlaid visualizations of multivariate spatial network data, enabling domain experts to explore thematic and physical data at multiple scales without programming barriers.  
More at [https://urbantk.org/streetweave](/streetweave/).
