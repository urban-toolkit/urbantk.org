Atmos is a declarative grammar for atmospheric data visualization. Grounded in a systematic survey of the atmospheric visualization literature, Atmos defines a formal vocabulary and compositional rules for describing atmospheric visualizations in terms of domain-native concepts independently of any rendering backend.
