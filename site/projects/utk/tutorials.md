## Examples

- [Multiple datasets](https://github.com/urban-toolkit/utk/blob/master/docs/examples/1-data.md): Aggregating multiple datasets considering the ZIP code boundaries of Manhattan. We also define a parallel coordinates chart to support the exploration.
- [Shadow in Downtown Manhattan](https://github.com/urban-toolkit/utk/blob/master/docs/examples/2-shadow.md): Visualize how shadows can have a significant impact on a highly dense region of New York City.
- [What-if scenarios](https://github.com/urban-toolkit/utk/blob/master/docs/examples/3-what-if.md): Defining a what-if scenario in downtown Chicago. The example uses simulation data from two different scenarios to analyze the impact a few buildings can have on Millennium Park.

Check [here](https://github.com/urban-toolkit/utk/tree/master/docs/examples) for a complete list of examples.
